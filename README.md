## 2. Interactive & Custom Homepage Features

1. **Interactive Hero Climate Mode Controller:** Visitors can switch between *Split & Ducted Cooling*, *Gas Ducted Heating*, and *Commercial VRV/VRF* to preview system capacities, pricing highlights, and ambient thermal accents.
2. **Official 7-Tier Split System Capacity & Price Calculator:** Built directly from Air Concepts Australia's official `/services` price schedule (`2.5kW` at `$2,000` through `9.4kW` at `$4,125` supplied and installed, including electrical supply from the main switchboard and a 5-Year Warranty) and 4 room-size brackets (`Small up to 20m²` to `Extra Large 60+m²`).
3. **Commercial HVAC, Builders & AroFlo Portal Showcase:** Highlights in-house mechanical engineering, full Victorian trade licence compliance, and a live preview of the AroFlo customer portal checklist.
4. **Authentic Melbourne Project Gallery & Lightbox:** Showcases 10 genuine installation and commissioning photographs from `airconceptsaustralia.com.au` across filterable categories with full-screen keyboard-accessible lightbox inspection.
5. **Verified Google Review Proof:** Features Paul Burgess's 5-star Google review alongside the original review screenshot from the live site.
6. **Interactive Melbourne Suburb & Postcode Checker:** Instant search across Ravenhall HQ and all 21 listed priority service locations within the 100km Melbourne CBD radius, with 1-click quote pre-filling.
7. **Transparent Static Quote Builder:** Pre-fills project details from the calculator, service cards, or suburb chips and generates a formatted `mailto:` enquiry to `info@airconceptsaustralia.com.au` or clipboard summary without fake backend submission claims.
